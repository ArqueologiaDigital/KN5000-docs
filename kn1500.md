# Technics SX-KN1500 — the TLCS-900 outlier

The **SX-KN1500** (1996) is an early Technics arranger keyboard. Unlike the later
MN10300 models ([KN2400/KN2600](/kn2400-kn2600/), KN6000, KN6500,
[KN7000](/kn7000/)), it is built around a **Toshiba TLCS-900 (`TMP95C061`)** — the
same CPU lineage as the [SX-KN5000](/system-overview/). So in the MAME work it
belongs with `kn5000.cpp`, not the MN10300 `kn7000.cpp` family.

## The ROM dump — and why it needs a redump

A single mask ROM (marked **IC15**, `QSIGT3C16079`, date code 9649 = 1996) was
dumped into two 2 MB files: `…ic15` and `…ic15.rest`. An earlier skeleton driver
mapped them as two logical ROMs:

| File | Region | CPU address |
|------|--------|-------------|
| `…ic15` | `prog` (program) | `0xe00000` |
| `…ic15.rest` | `rhythm` | `0xc00000` |

**The dump is flagged `BAD_DUMP`: it does not hold together.** Concretely:

- `…ic15` does **not** disassemble as coherent TLCS-900 — its odd byte positions are
  almost all `0xff` (yielding runs of `swi 7` / `ld W,0xff`), and the reset-vector
  region at the top is empty (`0xff`).
- The two files are not a clean complementary pair either: ~666K byte positions have
  data in *both*, and `…ic15.rest` is entirely `0xff` in its first 1 MB.

That `0xff`-in-alternate-lanes signature is what you get when only **one byte-lane of a
16-bit device** is captured (or the read is otherwise incomplete). So the images do
not currently form a working ROM — **a fresh dump of IC15 is needed** before the
KN1500 can be studied further.

## MAME status

A preservation **skeleton** driver was drafted (TLCS-900 `TMP95C061`, for
`kn5000.cpp`), but it is **deferred** from the initial MAME submission — the dump
above must be validated (a redump) first. The recovered image hashes are recorded
here meanwhile:
